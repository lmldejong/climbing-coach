# Climbing Coach: install the app on your phone

Climbing Coach is a training app that runs in your phone's browser. You add it to your home screen like a normal app. No app store and no account are needed, and it works offline at the gym or crag once installed.

## 1. Open your link
Your coach sends you a personal link that ends in your athlete code, for example:

`https://lmldejong.github.io/climbing-coach/athlete-1/`

Use **your own** link. Each link is a separate app with its own plan.

## 2. Add it to your home screen

**iPhone or iPad (Safari)**
1. Open your link in **Safari**. Other browsers on iPhone can't install it.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open your link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right), then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm with **Install**.

From now on, **always open Climbing Coach from the home-screen icon**. On iPhone in particular, the installed app keeps its own data, separate from Safari.

## 3. First start
1. **Privacy notice.** Read it, tick the consent box and tap **Agree and continue**. The app saves nothing until you agree.
2. **Setup tab.** Enter your name (it stays on your phone), your availability and anything your coach asked for. Check the training days and start date your coach gave you.
3. **Moving from another device?** On the old device, open Setup → **Export backup**. Then, in the installed app on the new device, open Setup → **Import backup** and pick that file.

## 4. Each training session
1. Open the app. **Train** shows today's session. Tap the session code to pick another session from this week.
2. Answer the three check-in questions (fatigue, finger pain, stiffness). Be honest: the plan adapts to them.
3. Work through the exercises. Use **Start** for the timer, rate your effort when you finish each one, and record attempts, minutes or kg × reps when asked.
4. At checkout, rate the whole session and your fingers.

If something hurts sharply, or you feel a pop or see swelling, stop and contact your coach or a physio. The app tells you the same.

## 5. Every week: send your week to your coach
After your last session of the week (usually Thursday):
1. On the Train page, tap **Export week and send to coach**, then **Send week to coach**.
2. Your phone's share menu opens. Choose **WhatsApp**, **Signal** or another end-to-end-encrypted messenger, then pick your coach.

The file contains your training records under your athlete code, not your name or profile.

## 6. Keep a backup (once a month)
Setup → **Export backup**, and save the file somewhere safe (for example, in Files or Drive). The backup does contain your profile; it is for you, so don't send it to others.

## Good to know
- **Updates** arrive automatically the next time you open the app with an internet connection. Your data is not affected.
- **Don't clear the browser data or website data** for this site. That deletes your records (import your backup to restore them).
- **One phone:** use the app on one device. Records don't sync between devices.
- **Stop or delete:** Setup → **Withdraw consent & delete my data** removes everything from this phone. Ask your coach to delete the files you sent too.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| My records are gone | Make sure you opened the home-screen icon, not a browser tab. Otherwise use Setup → Import backup. |
| No "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) | You are not in Safari. Copy the link into Safari. |
| No "Install app" (Android) | Use Chrome, then ⋮ → Add to Home screen. |
| The share menu doesn't open | The file downloads instead. Send it from your Downloads or Files app. |
| The app looks old | Open it once with internet, close it completely and open it again. |
